Movie Review

Forgotten Island

Synopsis

Forgotten Island is a 2026 DreamWorks animated film set in the 1990s Philippines that follows best friends Jo and Raissa as they discover a hidden portal to Nakali, a mystical island inhabited by Filipino folklore creatures that consumes human memories. Facing the threat of losing their shared history and pursued by supernatural forces like the Manananggal, the duo—aided by a weredog named Raww—must race against time to overcome Jo's secret and escape the island before they forget each other completely.
