Rovelyn Baterbonia explains ‘₱30 entrance fee’ at Baterbonia’s Collections store

Published September 29, 2026 08:34 AM PHT

Rovelyn Baterbonia, mother of late Ateneo de Manila University basketball player Rene Baterbonia, has asked supporters to commemorate her son’s 100th day of passing in a simple and prayerful way. Rene passed away on June 8 alongside his teammate Divine Adili in a drowning incident during their team-building activity in Dipaculao, Aurora. In a Facebook post on September 5, Rovelyn shared her request ahead of Rene’s commemoration. “Hi. Good morning sa lahat.
