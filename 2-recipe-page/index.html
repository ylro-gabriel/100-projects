Matcha Latte

Ingredients

Serving Size (38g)/63CC Scoop

Makes 8 fl oz (240ml) drink

  • 5 g matcha powder
  • 200 ml milk
  • sweetener (optional)

Steps

  1. Strain the matcha for a uniform consistency.
  2. Whisk the matcha using a frotter or manually.
  3. Pour milk into the glass with cube ice.
  4. Carefully pour the matcha into the glass.
  5. (optional) Add sweetener of your preference.

Preparation time

~5-7 minutes
